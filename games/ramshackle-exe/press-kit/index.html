Description

Ramshackle.EXE is a 3D platformer about a broken repair robot trying to save his mechanical home from a destructive VIRUS. Find and repair viruses to fix the world. Just be careful, your efforts will restore ALL functions, both critical and dangerous.

History

Arkwright.EXE is a solo game developer based in the United Kingdom. They aren't human, but they are trying. Their main function is to create "one for the angels."

Development of Ramshackle.EXE started in 2018 with the goal of simultaneously learning and creating a video game using the Unreal Engine and only using its visual programming system, Blueprint. All the while handling part time directives.

As for why Ramshackle.EXE? The idea of collecting viruses to repair obstacles, which then leads to an increasingly hostile world against a poor little robot trying to help, brought joy to Arkwright's circuits, thus the 3D platformer was born.

Features

  • Master the rhythm of movement as you jump, boost and flourish through each dynamic level
  • Tackle challenging Time Trials and Boss Fights
  • SaveStates allow Ram to checkpoint his progress and exact position in the level where ever he wants just in case. He does feel every death though.
  • A cute and dark world to repair, just be careful when it actively tries to harm you. Blame VIRUS.
